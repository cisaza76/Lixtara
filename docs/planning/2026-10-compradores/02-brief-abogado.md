## 3. Rules we understand apply (please confirm or correct)
- **NAR MLS Policy Statement 8.13** (since 2024-08-17):
  - a written agreement before touring (in person or live virtual);
  - compensation objectively ascertainable, not open-ended;
  - no compensation from any source above the agreed amount;
  - a conspicuous statement that compensation is not set by law and is fully negotiable.
- **F.S. 475.278:** transaction broker (presumed) or single agent; no dual agency; single-agent disclosure before showing; written consent to transition.
- **FAC 61J2-10.025** (advertising) and **61J2-10.028** (sharing compensation with a party, with full disclosure).
- **2026 NAR Code of Ethics changes:** Art. 7 and SOP 17-4.
- **MIAMI MLS Rules 2026:** we have requested the current text from MIAMI.

## 5. Transaction scenarios to review

**A. Lixtara represents a buyer purchasing a property listed by another brokerage.**
- Compensation is individually agreed at 2 / 2.5 / 3 %.
- **Shortfall policy (proposed):**
  1. Negotiate the difference in the offer.
  2. If refused, the broker may agree in writing to reduce Lixtara's compensation.
  3. Otherwise the buyer may voluntarily cover the difference, within the maximum obligation accepted in the agreement.
  4. The buyer may walk away from that property.
- Lixtara never absorbs it automatically, and the buyer never faces a surprise at closing.

**B2. Lixtara represents both seller (listing) and buyer in the same transaction.**
- Proposed total cap = listing commission per plan + **1.5 % buyer side**: Essentials 2 %, Pro 2.5 %, Concierge 3 % (plus the fixed platform fee).
- This applies only with prior written consent of both parties, in the listing agreement and in the Showing Agreement.
- Listing commissions remain independent obligations unless amended in writing. **Existing listing agreements are not changed automatically.**
- The 1.5 % applies only to Lixtara's own listings, not to scenario A.
- Who pays the buyer side (seller and/or buyer) is defined in writing before the tour.

**B4. An unrepresented buyer purchases a Lixtara listing.**
- Lixtara works only for the seller and provides no buyer representation services.
- Please confirm the required disclosure, and the relationship our current listing agreement creates with the seller.
