**Part 1. Requests for written confirmation**

**1. A search assistant that never receives Licensed Content (Section III.B.4).**
We are evaluating an optional assistant on our property search page. The buyer describes, in their own words, what they are looking for (for example "a 3-bedroom house in Broward under $600,000"). The assistant turns that description into search parameters: county, city, ZIP, price range, bedrooms, bathrooms and property type. Those parameters are then executed by our standard search engine against the IDX data.

The AI model:
- **never receives** listing data, photos, search results, result counts or any information derived from the listings;
- only processes the text typed by the consumer and the search parameters the consumer has chosen.

Results are displayed by our regular website code, with full attribution and disclosures, and are never sent back to the model.

*Question:* Can you confirm in writing whether this design is permitted under Section III.B.4, or whether MIAMI considers it "feeding Licensed Content directly or indirectly" to an AI system? If any additional safeguards are required, please let us know.

**2. Saved-search email alerts.**

*Question:* Is this permitted for an IDX participant whose agreement names a single website? If so, may the email include listing content (photo, price, attribution), or must it contain only a link back to lixtara.com?
